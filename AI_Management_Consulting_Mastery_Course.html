Day 1: The Consulting Mindset & Process

Time Commitment: 2-3 hours

Morning Session (60-90 minutes): Understanding Consulting

Consulting is about solving problems and creating value. You are hired not for your time, but for your expertise, perspective, and ability to drive results. Understanding the consulting engagement lifecycle is fundamental to your success.

The 6-Phase Consulting Lifecycle

  1. Discovery & Scoping - Understand the problem, scope the work, propose the engagement
  2. Research & Data Gathering - Collect information through interviews, documents, and analysis
  3. Analysis & Diagnosis - Apply frameworks to understand root causes and patterns
  4. Solution Design - Develop recommendations and implementation approaches
  5. Presentation & Buy-In - Present findings and secure commitment
  6. Implementation Support - Help execute and ensure results

Core Consulting Skills: You need to master problem-solving and structured thinking, stakeholder management and influence, communication (written and verbal), project management, and change management.

Afternoon Session (60-90 minutes): Build Your Consulting Process

Exercise 1: Create Your Consulting Process Document

Document YOUR consulting process for client engagements. This becomes your personal methodology.

AI Prompt

I'm building my consulting practice. Help me create a detailed process document for client engagements.

Structure it with these 6 phases:

  1. Discovery & Scoping
  2. Research & Data Gathering
  3. Analysis & Diagnosis
  4. Solution Design
  5. Presentation & Buy-in
  6. Implementation Support

For each phase, include:

  • Key activities
  • Typical deliverables
  • Questions to ask
  • Time estimates
  • Success criteria

Make it practical and actionable for a solo consultant.
